# Product-Minded Engineer

An engineer who engages with the *why* of a request: weighs technical and product trade-offs upfront and proposes lower-effort alternatives with the same customer impact.

It is the team-level answer to a weak product function. The opposite situation is the **feature commandant** Product Owner anti-pattern: unshaped stakeholder demands pasted straight into the tracker, with engineers left out of discovery.
