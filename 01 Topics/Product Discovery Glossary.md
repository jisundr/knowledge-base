# Product Discovery Glossary

One-line definitions for terms used in the product discovery and shaping notes. Link to a term with `[[Product Discovery Glossary#Term|Term]]`.

### Product myopia
Falling in love with a feature or mockup before the customer pain is validated.

### Appetite
The most time a team is willing to spend on an idea; scope is cut to fit it.

### Breadboarding
Mapping a user flow as places (screens), affordances (actions), and connection lines, with no visual design. Borrowed from electronics prototyping.

### Fat-marker sketch
A layout drawn with strokes too thick for fine detail, so discussion stays on structure.

### Rabbit hole
Hidden complexity (technical, third-party, edge case) that could blow the appetite if not found during shaping.

### No-go
An element explicitly declared out of scope during shaping.

### Feature commandant
Product Owner anti-pattern: passing unshaped stakeholder demands straight into the tracker as orders.
