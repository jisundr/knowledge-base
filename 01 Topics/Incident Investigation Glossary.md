# Incident Investigation Glossary

One-line definitions for terms used in the incident notes. Link to a term with `[[Incident Investigation Glossary#Term|Term]]`.

### ICAM
Incident Cause Analysis Method: groups contributing factors into absent or failed defences, individual/team actions, task/environmental conditions, and organisational factors.

### 5 Whys
Asking "why?" repeatedly (about five times) to move from a symptom toward a cause. Tends toward one linear chain, so pair it with branching analysis.

### OSHA Form 301
U.S. OSHA "Injury and Illness Incident Report": the per-incident record employers must complete for recordable injuries.

### ISO 45001
International standard for occupational health and safety management systems.

### UK HSE
Health and Safety Executive: the UK regulator for workplace health and safety. (HSE in "HSE operations" means Health, Safety, and Environment.)

### EU AI Act risk tiers
Common shorthand for the EU AI Act's structure: prohibited practices, high-risk systems (Annex III), systems with transparency duties (Art. 50, often called "limited risk", though the Act does not use that label), and everything else. Annex III point 4 lists employment uses as high risk: decisions on work terms, promotion or termination, task allocation based on behaviour or traits, and monitoring or evaluating workers.

### Local rationality
The principle that people's actions made sense to them given what they knew and saw at the time.

### Counterfactual language
Wording about what did not happen ("should have", "failed to"). It implies blame and hides why the actual actions seemed reasonable.
