# Design Document Types

Separate documents by the question each answers, so none tries to be permanent truth for everything.

| Document | Answers | Lifespan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PRD** | *What* and *why*, from the product view: problems, goals, non-goals, success metrics | Frozen once committed |
| **RFC** | *How* to solve a complex or cross-cutting problem: options, trade-offs, rejected ideas | Until a decision is made |
| **Spec / change brief** | *Exactly what* this one piece of work does, and how to verify it | Scaffolding while in flight |
| **ADR** | *Which* single architectural choice was made, and its cost | Forever, until superseded |

See [[Architecture Decision Record (ADR)]] and [[Spec-Driven Development]].
