# Product Engineer

A role that combines product management and software engineering: discover user needs, write precise problem specs, and direct AI agents to write the code, instead of typing syntax.

## Lineage

1960s **systems analyst** (bridge between business needs and computing) → split into **product manager** and **software engineer** → merging back into the **product engineer** as agents take over implementation. Andrew Ng notes progressive teams blurring these roles: builders who understand users directly and write clear specs can have agents execute right away.

## Demand

Job postings for forward-deployed product engineers rose 800% in nine months, with average senior packages around $240,000 (figures as stated in the source).

## Core skills

- Framing [[Accidental vs Essential Complexity|essential complexity]]: understanding the domain and what "good" means.
- Turning domain knowledge into machine-verifiable specs and evals ([[Spec-Driven Development]], [[AI System Evaluation]]).
