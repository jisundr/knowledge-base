# Junior Developer Apprenticeship Gap

The entry-level pipeline breaking because agents now do the work juniors used to learn on.

- **Broken ladder**: juniors learned by taking well-defined, low-risk coding tickets, which are exactly the tasks now handed to agents.
- **Demographic shift**: Bureau of Labor Statistics data shows employment for engineers aged 22–25 down sharply since 2023, while demand for engineers aged 41–49 has grown.
- **Training deficit**: programs that grow product-minded builders (e.g., Google's Associate Product Manager program) produce only a small fraction of the people needed each year.

## Response

Build deliberate new career paths that train juniors in product discovery and spec design, the work of a [[Product Engineer]], rather than in ticket implementation.
