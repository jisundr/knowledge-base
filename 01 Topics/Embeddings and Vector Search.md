# Embeddings and Vector Search

Keyword matching is brittle. Embeddings convert text (from PDFs or other formats) into numerical vectors, so systems capture **semantic meaning** rather than exact word matches.

Vector databases then search by context and concept similarity, e.g., recognizing that "Kubernetes" relates to "containers" and "orchestration".
