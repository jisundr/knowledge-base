# Product Trio

A cross-functional group that owns discovery together, each person covering one kind of risk:

| Role | Accountable for |
| --- | --- |
| Product manager | Business viability and value |
| Product designer (UX) | Usability and workflow ergonomics |
| Tech lead / architect | Technical feasibility and architectural integrity |

The trio checks ideas against the [[Four Big Product Risks]] continuously, not once at the start. Leaving engineers out of discovery leads to Product Owner anti-patterns; see [[Product-Minded Engineer]].
