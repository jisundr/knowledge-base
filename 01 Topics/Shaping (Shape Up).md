# Shaping (Shape Up)

The step from Basecamp's *Shape Up* method that turns a raw idea into a bounded concept **before** anyone commits engineering time or assigns tasks to agents. The output is concrete enough to build, and loose enough that designers and developers still make the detailed decisions.

## Principles

- **Appetite, not estimate**: decide the most time the idea is worth (e.g., a 2-week small batch or a 6-week big batch), then cut scope to fit. Fixed time, variable scope.
- **Breadboarding**: map the flow as places, affordances, and connection lines, with no visual design.
- **Fat-marker sketches**: layouts drawn too thick for detail, so nobody argues about pixels.
- **Rabbit holes and no-gos**: find hidden complexity (API limits, edge cases) early, and declare what is out of bounds.

Terms are defined in [[Product Discovery Glossary]]. A shaped idea is the input to [[Spec-Driven Development]].
