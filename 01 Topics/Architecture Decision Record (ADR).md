# Architecture Decision Record (ADR)

A short, numbered, immutable record of one significant architectural choice and its cost, stored in the repo (e.g., `docs/adr/ADR-0014-store-session-state-in-postgres.md`). It is never edited after acceptance; a later ADR supersedes it.

Write one only for load-bearing decisions: an expensive trade-off, a new library, or a break from established patterns. Common templates are the Nygard format and MADR (see [[SDLC Documentation Glossary]]).

ADRs are where rationale survives after specs are retired under a [[Spec Exit Strategy]].
